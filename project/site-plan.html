HoopHub Community Club

W05 Project Site Plan | Author: Rich Kevin Panozo Vargas

1. Site Name

Selected Name: HoopHub Community Club

Reason: This name represents a centralized hub and community club for basketball players and fans. It clearly communicates the core purpose of gathering local basketball enthusiasts, sharing league updates, and promoting community tournaments.

Optional Domain Availability: hoophub-club.org

2. Site Purpose

The website functions as a central informational portal for the local basketball community. It provides schedules for upcoming recreational games, tournament brackets, training tips, and an online membership/sign-up form for players wanting to join local teams.

3. Scenarios

Target audience users will visit the site looking for specific answers. Key user scenarios include:

  • What are the registration requirements and deadlines to join the upcoming seasonal tournament?
  • Where can I check the updated weekly game schedules, court locations, and live scores?

4. Color Scheme

The site utilizes a clean, sporty contrast palette designed to maintain high readability and visual identity:

Primary Color: Navy Blue (#1b263b)
Used for: Headers, footers, and primary navigation.
Secondary Color: Basketball Orange (#e85d04)
Used for: Accent lines, buttons, and titles.

5. Typography

Typography has been chosen to provide a modern, clean athletic aesthetic while ensuring optimal legibility:

  • Heading Font: 'Poppins' (Sans-serif, bold, used for main headings and titles to give a dynamic structure).
  • Body Font: 'Roboto' (Sans-serif, clean, highly readable, used for paragraphs, lists, and content descriptions).
